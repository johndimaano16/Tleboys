About TLE Boys

Founded by John Dimaano, TLE Boys was born out of a deep passion for cars and the drive to make car care more accessible. Cars are more than just machines—they are a reflection of personal style, freedom, and hard work. My lifelong enthusiasm for cars inspired me to create a service that brings professional car care right to your doorstep.

At TLE Boys, we are committed to delivering top-tier mobile car services with precision and care. Our services range from mobile car washes and detailed cleanings to essential maintenance like oil changes and tire services. We use premium products and expert techniques to ensure your car looks and performs its best.

Our mission is simple: combine our passion for cars with professional service to save you time and keep your vehicle in peak condition.
